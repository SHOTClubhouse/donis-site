How it works

The rules

Format2v2

Games last 8 minutes. Squads of up to four: two on, two subs. Played on concrete in street shoes.

Points3 · 1 · 0

Three for a win, one for a draw. Only finished games count in the table.

Tie-breakGD

Level on points goes to goal difference, then goals scored.

On the pitch

01

Kick-offs

One player from each team stands back to back on the centre spot with the ball between them. On the whistle, turn and battle for it.

02

Kick-ins

Ball out over the touchline: the attacking team kicks it in from where it went out. Pass or dribble it in. Defenders stand at least 3 yards away.

03

Corners

Ball out over the byline: the attacking team takes it from the nearest corner mark. Pass or dribble it in. Defenders stand at least 3 yards away.

04

Penalty area

No defender can stay in the box for more than 5 seconds in a row. If they do, the referee gives the other team a penalty.

05

Penalties

A 1v1 showdown. The attacker starts in their own half and has 10 seconds from the whistle to score. One defender starts on the goal line.

06

Cards

Red and yellow cards as usual. Any unsportsmanlike conduct means a one-minute sin bin, so your team plays 2v1.
