About Donis

A name with family behind it. A club built for the people who make football culture.

The name

Family in the name

Donis is named in honour of a grandad, with a nod to the Greek numeral for 16. Its roots are personal; the invitation is open to everyone who feels part of this culture.

Our mission

Make space for the next generation

We bring street football, music and style together to create places to play, belong and be seen. From events to local communities, Donis aims to open more opportunities in inner cities and seaside towns.
